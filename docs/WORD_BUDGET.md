# Word budget

The reconciled plan is 200,000 Vietnamese words. `budget_audit()` reports no mismatches.

| Unit | Count | How the sum is built |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume | 1 | 200,000 |
| Chapters, including prologue, one interlude, and epilogue | 11 | 8,000 + 22,000 × 6 + 8,000 + 24,000 + 22,000 + 20,000 + 8,000 |
| Parts | 52 | Each part target is 3,200, 3,500, 4,000, 6,000, or 10,000 |
| Scenes | 224 | Scene targets sum to their part |
| Beats | 784 | Each beat is 150–350 words and the beats sum to their scene |

Eight main chapters are 20,000–24,000 words. One part, “Đêm món đồ suýt rời kho”, is 10,000 words. Standard parts are 3,200–4,000. Nothing was shortened to make a failed draft look finished.

Prologue 8,000, interlude 8,000, and epilogue 8,000 are framing units with their own targets. They are not major chapters, and they are required before the volume can be marked complete.

If a chapter is dropped because a required plotline is missing, the allocated sum falls below 200,000 and `budget_problems` records the gap. The plan does not rescale the remaining chapters to hide that gap.

Local source text is still the Volume 16 study notes. Volumes 1–16 are not ingested.
